02 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Modality Scope

Overview of evaluated modalities and benchmark data.

Text Forensics & Model Attribution

02.1

Probes structural discourse continuity using bidirectional cloze infilling and bipartite matching to attribute generated text without token log-probabilities. Evaluated across 10 global languages (98.92% cross-lingual AUROC).

Benchmark: ClozeCongruence 3.0 / 4.0 • SSRN / Zenodo

Vision Forensics (Diffusion Detection)

02.2

Applies deterministic VAE latent space inversion and 2D-FFT azimuthal spectral integration to detect transposed convolution lattice harmonics across Midjourney, SDXL, DALL-E 3, and FLUX in <0.02s.

Benchmark: Latent Resonance • Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22158286

Audio Forensics (Voice Clones & Vocoders)

02.3

Evaluates physical phase cancellation and comb filtering anomalies resulting from transposed neural vocoder upsampling in synthetic speech and generative music (EnCodec, DAC).

Benchmark: Neural Codec Resonance • Preprint

Financial AI Security

02.4

Black-box attribution of proprietary fine-tuned financial LLMs in banking disclosures and AML report synthesis without logit leakage.

Accepted: ACM ICAIF 2026 FinSec Workshop
