About Me & The Journey

01 / Person & Background
The Dual Identity

Not an Academic by Profession, but a Thinker by Night

To be clear: I do not introduce myself as a full-time academic or career researcher. By day, I am a Lead Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) and AI Quality Engineer with over 11 years of enterprise experience architecting test automation frameworks, non-deterministic evaluation harnesses, and LLM quality gates across aerospace and enterprise GenAI Centers of Excellence.

When my day-job deliverables are complete and the house goes quiet, I turn to my true hobby: independent scientific research. I am captivated by the unprecedented AI capabilities humanity has built over the last few years. I don’t do this for institutional tenure or grant funding — I do it mostly for the pure fun of discovery and with the humble hope of adding a small, meaningful contribution to the global scientific community.

The bravest decision I have ever made was pursuing my Master of Technology (M.Tech) in AR/VR and Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur (IIT Jodhpur) while working full-time in demanding enterprise engineering leadership roles.

Balancing rigorous IIT graduate coursework, mathematical foundations, and research projects with high-stakes production software deliverables was a herculean effort. It demanded extreme discipline and sleepless weekends, but it proved to me that curiosity and persistence can conquer any cognitive limit.

Beyond The Terminal

Outside of code and research papers, I am deeply connected to the arts and literature. I love singing and listening to music across classical, Indian, and contemporary genres.

02 / Epistemology
Origin Story

How the Cloze Congruence Intuition Just Worked

My initial AI text detection paper was never planned as a grand theoretical treatise. It was an evolved, vanilla side project I was tinkering with while learning non-deterministic evaluation and black-box verification methods for LLMs.

I asked myself a simple question: “If an LLM generated this prose, will another foundation model reconstruct masked macro-discursive voids with an unnatural, deterministic resonance compared to human prose?”

Coincidentally, the intuition just worked. I validated it twice, thrice, and across dozens of prompt permutations — every single time, the statistical separation between AI infilling coherence and human expressive entropy held true. That vanilla experiment matured into multi-pass sentence masking, Kuhn-Munkres Hungarian bipartite matching on permutation group $S_3$, and continuous resonance spectroscopy.

The Future of Work

Why AI Expands Human Creativity in Science

Living through this research journey has completely solidified my worldview on artificial intelligence:

“I am convinced now that with the advent of AI into our personal lives, a normal engineer can substantially contribute to the research field while working alongside a capable AI.”

I firmly believe AI will not take our jobs. It will not render human thought obsolete. Instead, it will drastically amplify our cognitive reach. What humanity is about to witness is a tremendous explosion of human creativity, intuitive hypothesis testing, and unconventional problem-solving in science.
